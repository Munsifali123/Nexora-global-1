# Backlink Plan

Status: planning only. No outreach, submissions, paid placements, or link exchanges have been performed.

## First approach

Create a genuinely useful, source-cited solar system size estimator only after the current guide cluster has accumulated enough Search Console and lead-funnel data to justify it. That asset is the strongest prospective link target because it is useful to homeowners, local energy educators, and property-focused publishers without requiring thin “link bait” content.

## Prospecting criteria

Prioritize only relevant, editorially maintained sources:

1. Local clean-energy, homeowner, and consumer-education resources serving the markets Nexora can actually support.
2. Reputable renovation, real-estate, sustainability, and energy-efficiency publishers that can evaluate a practical sizing resource.
3. Industry associations and community organizations with resource libraries where eligibility and editorial review are explicit.

Avoid directory spam, reciprocal-link schemes, paid links that pass ranking value, automated submissions, and irrelevant high-metric sites.

## Preparation before any outreach

- Confirm the estimator is accurate, mobile-friendly, source-cited, and has a clear methodology.
- Prepare one concise value proposition per audience; do not use mass templates.
- Verify business coverage, compliance claims, and destination ownership.
- Track prospects, contact date, response, live URL, link attribute, referral sessions, and qualified leads in an approved CRM or sheet.

## Success measures

Evaluate quality over volume: relevant referring domains, referral engagement, branded search growth, organic impressions to linked assets, and qualified property inquiries. Review monthly; stop approaches that do not produce relevant traffic or editorial placements.
