# Backlink Research — Nexora Global

Research date: 2026-08-11. Scope: research and qualification only. No contact, submission, account creation, placement negotiation, payment, or guest-post publication occurred.

## Positioning guardrail

Nexora is a US solar inquiry-and-matching service for property owners. It is not an installer, lender, utility, government body, engineering adviser, or incentive authority. Any future pitch must therefore offer independent homeowner value—useful methodology, a cited calculator, or aggregated first-party insight—not provider claims, savings guarantees, or financing advice.

## Method and confidence

The CSV contains 111 topical public prospects desk-qualified from their visible editorial/resource focus. Scores are qualitative (1–10) and intended to order work, not to represent domain metrics. Exact backlink counts, Domain Rating, traffic, and acceptance likelihood were not claimed because no backlink database was available. Before outreach, re-validate the specific page, current editorial policy, market coverage, and contact route.

Public research confirms the consumer-resource ecosystem: DOE’s homeowner solar guide and rooftop-potential page cite consumer tools including EnergySage and NREL PVWatts. Renewable Energy World’s public submission guidance requires original, non-promotional, technical or data-led material. Solar Builder publishes contributed reporting. These are patterns, not assurances that those sites will link to Nexora.

## Resource-page strategy

The strongest resource-page prospects are Solar United Neighbors, MREA, CESA, IREC, Home Energy Magazine, Green Building Advisor, Build with Rise, Rewiring America, DOE-adjacent consumer-resource ecosystems, and state efficiency programs. Government/university pages are research references, not link targets: do not assume they accept commercial resources.

Current guides are useful decision content, but the future estimator would be materially more linkable only if it discloses assumptions, cites public sources, avoids savings guarantees, works without lead capture, and links to a methodology page.

## Competitor link gaps — confirmed vs. hypothesis

Confirmed public examples: DOE’s solar-rooftop-potential and homeowner-guide pages cite EnergySage and NREL PVWatts as consumer resources. Nexora is not cited. This is not an outreach opportunity now; it demonstrates the evidence bar for a tool.

Potential gaps requiring a backlink database before outreach: resource pages, finance explainers, and home-energy publishers that cite multiple competitor tools but not Nexora. No unverified source was labeled as a confirmed linker. Use Ahrefs, Semrush, or Moz only in a later approved validation pass.

## Unlinked brand mentions

No verified public mention of `nexoraglobal.agency` by a third party was found in this pass. Search results contain similarly named but unrelated crypto, software, telecom, logistics, and investment businesses. Do not pursue them as reclamation opportunities.

## Broken-link opportunities

No verified broken-link replacement has been recorded. Do not run broken-link outreach until a page is found with a demonstrably broken relevant solar/tool link and Nexora has a genuinely equivalent or better replacement.

## 90-day authority roadmap — proposal only

- **Weeks 1–2:** re-validate the Top 20, remove poor fits, confirm coverage and compliance, and decide whether the estimator brief is approved. No outreach without approval.
- **Weeks 3–4:** after approval, pursue a small set of personalized, high-fit editorial/resource contacts; track every action and outcome.
- **Month 2:** approved expert contributions and resource outreach, only where the asset/data genuinely fits.
- **Month 3:** launch the approved linkable asset or a privacy-reviewed digital-PR data story; measure links, referral quality, organic impressions, and qualified inquiries.

## Measurement system

Track relevant referring domains, editorial links, referral sessions and engagement, Search Console impressions/clicks/queries, branded search, ranking movement, referral leads, and organic leads. Do not use Domain Authority alone as a success metric.
